About PTECH SOLUTIONS

PTECH SOLUTIONS is a sole proprietorship operated by Prabhdeep Singh. We provide web development and technical services to clients on a direct-project basis. Each project is discussed with the customer, scoped according to their requirements, quoted individually and delivered electronically.

Our work focuses on website setup and customization, WordPress services, PHP upgrades, WHMCS configuration and related technical support.
